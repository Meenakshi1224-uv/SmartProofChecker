Learn Proof Techniques

Master core mathematical thinking with clear explanations and structured examples for direct proof, induction, contradiction, and contraposition.

Direct Proof

Start from the assumptions and combine definitions, algebra, and logic to reach the conclusion.

Induction

Prove the base case and then show that if a statement holds for k, it also holds for k + 1.

Contradiction

Assume the opposite of the claim, derive an impossible result, and conclude the claim must be true.

Contrapositive

Prove a logically equivalent form: if not Q then not P.
